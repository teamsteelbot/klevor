Drastic changes were made to this layer, focusing mainly in modifying the layer's thickness to reduce weight. Also, strategically placed holes were added, their main purpose is to reduce the robot's weight and to ease the assembly process. The entire base was completely custom-made, designed specifically to adapt to the dimensions and requirements from each of the elements that we are using in the robot.

This part has faced a lot of changes when compared to our second prototype's layer. Mainly, we had to reduce its dimensions and weight to make sure Klevor didn't go above the weight limit. On this layer, we placed several important component such as the [Shargeek Storm 2](../../electronic/components/current.en.md#shargeek-storm-2) and the [Raspberry Pi 5](../../electronic/components/current.en.md#raspberry-pi-5-16gb-ram) so the design adapted to provide a safe support and a proper integration for these components.
