This Digital Multimeter with a USB-C Port is a very versatile tool that is capable of measuring both the voltage and the amperage for multiple devices that implement this type of connection. It's pretty useful to check our battery's state, the charging efficiency and overall health of our electronics.

In our case, this was especially useful whenever we wanted to check that our Raspberry Pi 5 was receiving the correct amperage through the USB-C port, assuring an optimal device performance. Also, its capacity to measure voltage and amperage in real-time facilitates the diagnosis for any electronics issue within any of our projects..
